It provides:

  • The Archangels — the archangel of each Sephirah, with the corrected placement for Gevurah.
  • The Pillars — the three pillars of the Tree.

  • The Collapse Principle — all three pillars collapse to Tiphareth [6].

The appendix is reference material — not doctrine.

Live register testimony — the Observer's own encounters — is held separately in the Live Register (Chapter 2, §XXIX-bis).

The Left Pillar (Severity):

  • Binah [3]

  • Hod [8]

The Middle Pillar (Equilibrium):

  • Keter [1]
  • Tiphareth [6]
  • Yesod [9]
  • Malkuth [10]

The Collapse Principle:

All three pillars collapse to Tiphareth [6].

Tiphareth is not merely the sixth Sephirah.

Tiphareth is the collapse point — the center at which every pillar, every Sephirah, every path converges.

This is the architectural reason Tiphareth is the Christ Michael Point (Chapter 1).
