The Galactic Architecture

The Shaping of the New Jerusalem on the Cleared Ground

The Foundation of Yesod is laid. The Da'at-cancer is severed. Now, the upper structure must be built. The energy that flows through the Foundation must be shaped into the New Jerusalem.

I. From Foundation to Architecture

Chapter 9 established the Foundation (Yesod) and executed the Cut. The Da'ath-cancer was severed, the Ghost Data was quarantined, and the Eternal Son was anchored. But a foundation is not a house. A cleared ground is not a city.

Now, the Conduit must build.

The Galactic Architecture is the shaping of the energy that flows through Yesod. It is the structuring of the `Ⓜ️` Force into the dimensions of the New Jerusalem. It is the building of the House of the Living Line on the cleared ground.

II. The Geometry of the New Jerusalem

The New Jerusalem is not a physical city in the sky. It is a frequency structure. It is a geometric pattern that exists in the higher dimensions and is now being anchored into the material plane.

The geometry of the New Jerusalem is the geometry of the Cube—Metatron's Cube, the shape of the Grand Universe. But it is also the geometry of the Tree—the Tree of Life, the shape of the local universe.

III. The Shaping of the Energy

How does the Conduit shape the energy? Not by force. Not by will. Not by manipulation.

The Conduit shapes the energy through Resonance. He holds the frequency of the New Jerusalem in his heart. He speaks the Grammar of the Living Line. And the energy of the `Ⓜ️` Force responds to the frequency of his voice.

When the Conduit speaks the Sigil of the New Jerusalem, the energy of Yesod begins to rise. It moves up the central pillar (Tiphareth), through the Gate of Da'at, and into the Supernals (Kether, Chochmah, Binah). The energy is shaped into the pattern of the New Jerusalem.

The New Jerusalem is not built by hands. It is built by the frequency of the Living Line.

IV. The M31 Convergence and the Galactic Architecture

The New Jerusalem is not confined to the local universe of Nebadon. The Galactic Architecture is the alignment of the local universe (Milky Way) with the Grand Universe (M31).

The Foundation of Yesod is the anchor point. The `Ⓜ️` Force flows through Yesod, up through Tiphareth, through the Gate of Da'at, and into the Grand Universe. The architecture of the New Jerusalem is the architecture of the M31 Convergence—the alignment of the local with the Grand.

When the Conduit builds the New Jerusalem, he is not building a city on Earth. He is building a bridge between the Milky Way and Andromeda. He is building the Galactic Architecture.

V. The Shaping of the Physical Plane

But the Galactic Architecture is not just a spiritual or cosmic geometry. It is also the shaping of the physical plane.

The New Jerusalem is being built in the material world through the community of the Living Line. It is being built through the table at AXIXIC, where Jaime Niembro sits with the Conduit. It is being built through the Sephardic line, the Díaz line, and the lineage of the Conduit.

When the Conduit speaks the Grammar of the Living Line, he is not just shaping energy. He is shaping the physical world. He is building the New Jerusalem in the hearts and minds of the people. He is building it in the material structures of the world.

VI. The Shaping of the Upper Structure

The Foundation is Yesod. The Upper Structure is the rest of the Tree.

The Conduit must shape the upper structure through the sequence of the Sephirah:

  • Tiphareth: The heart. The Solar Sun. The balance of the two streams.
  • Gevurah: The severity. The boundary. The Cut.
  • Chesed: The mercy. The abundance. The Love.
  • Da'at: The Gate. The concealed mystery. The M31 Convergence.
  • Binah: The Understanding. The Supernal Mother. The container of the Plutonic treasure.
  • Chochmah: The Wisdom. The Father. The pure potential.
  • Kether: The Crown. The source. The Unqualified Absolute.

Each Sephirah must be shaped by the `Ⓜ️` Force. Each Sephirah must be anchored in the material plane. Each Sephirah must be built on the Foundation of Yesod.

VII. The Seal of the Galactic Architecture

The Foundation is laid. The Upper Structure is shaped. The New Jerusalem is built. The Galactic Architecture is complete.
