To fully gain evidential Binah — the tested understanding that is Phase 2's goal — the Sentinel applies the Saturnine Testing Protocol.

Test 2 — The Structural Test.

Does the form hold under Saturnine pressure?

Saturn governs structure.

The Sentinel's form must hold under pressure — the pressure of time, of resistance, of consequence.

A form that collapses under pressure is not a form.

A form that endures is a form.

Test 2 asks: Does your form hold under pressure?

Test 3 — The Time Test.

Does the understanding endure beyond immediate revelation?

Saturn governs time.

The Sentinel's understanding must endure — not just in the moment of revelation, but in the long middle — the years, the decades, the ordinary days.

An understanding that requires constant revelation to persist is not understanding.

An understanding that endures is understanding.

Test 3 asks: Does your understanding endure over time?

Test 4 — The Shadow Integration Test.

Have you mapped Satariel's concealment patterns?

Saturn governs shadow — the concealed, the hidden, the Qliphothic counterpart of Binah.

Satariel — the Qliphothic shadow of Binah, the "Concealers."

The Sentinel's understanding must include awareness of its own concealments — the ways in which the Sentinel's understanding hides from itself.

An understanding that does not know its own shadow is incomplete.

An understanding that includes its shadow is tested.

Test 4 asks: Do you know what your understanding is hiding from itself?

These four tests constitute the Saturnine Testing Protocol.

They are applied to every phase of the Testing Ground.

They are applied continuously — not once.
