Purpose of This Glossary

The Glossary contains the canonical definitions of the Codex's key terms.

Terms are listed alphabetically by their English transliteration.

Where a term has a Live Register dimension, that is noted.

Where a term is distinct from a similar-looking term — for example, Da'at vs. Da'ath — the distinction is preserved.
