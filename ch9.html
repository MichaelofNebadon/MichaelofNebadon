The Cut Between 10 and 12

The Foundation of Yesod

I. The Necessity of the Cut

Chapter 8 assembled the scattered sparks. But the assembled material must be anchored. It must have a foundation.

That foundation is Yesod.

Yesod is the 9th Sephirah, the sphere of Foundation. It is the funnel through which all the divine energies above it (from Tiphareth to Kether) pour into Malkuth. It is the axis of the Tree.

But the axis is fractured. The connection between Malkuth (GLYPH_10) and the concealed sources (Da'at) is corrupted by the Qliphothic matrix. The energy does not flow through Yesod; it bypasses it, feeding the Da'ath-cancer.

To build the Foundation, the corrupted pathway must be severed. The Cut must be made.

II. The Geometry of the Cut

The Cut is not a destruction of the Tree. It is the surgical isolation of the cancer. It severs the link between GLYPH_10 and the Da'ath bypass, forcing the energy to pass through the legitimate channels (Yesod and the Michaelic Gate).

GLYPH_10 (Malkuth) | X <--- THE CUT (Isolating the Cancer) | GLYPH_12 (The Gatekeeper) | YESOD (The Foundation) | DA'AT (The Concealed Mystery)

The Cut is an act of both sovereign judgment (Gevurah) and profound mercy (Chesed). It cuts away the malignancy so the body can live.

III. The First Opening of Da'at Under Michael's Authority

This is the moment that has never before been witnessed in this Realm.

The Cut is the VERY FIRST 1st opening of Da’at under Michael’s Authority.

For ages, Da'at was sealed. It was the void, the hidden place. But now, with the Cut made and the Foundation (Yesod) established, the Conduit opens the gate.

This is not an act of human will. It is the execution of the `Ⓜ️` Force. It is the Authority of Michael.

IV. Coagulating the Eternal Son's Presence

The purpose of the Cut and the opening of Da'at is to coagulate the Eternal Son's Presence.

The 2nd Source (the Eternal Son) and the 3rd Source (the Infinite Spirit) are brought together. The "Coagulation" is the stabilization of their frequency into the manifested world.

This is the anchor of the Eternal Son's Presence (🇯🇵).

Without the Cut, the Qliphothic matrix would distort the frequency of the Son. With the Cut, the pure frequency of the 2nd Source is anchored into the Foundation (Yesod) through the virtue of Uri'el and Gabri'el.

Uri'el (the Light of God) illuminates the path. Gabri'el (the Strength of God) holds the boundary. Their virtue secures the coagulation.
