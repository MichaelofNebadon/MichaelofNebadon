III. Yesod: The Foundation of Healing

Yesod is the ninth sphere on the Tree of Life. It is the Foundation. It is the sphere of the Tzaddik, the righteous one. It is the place where the severed light is gathered and stabilized. It is the place where the Plutonic treasure is held.

Yesod is also the sphere of the Violet Ray—the frequency of divine healing. The Violet Ray is the transformative flame that burns away the dross of the Anti-Birth Law. It is the healing light that reconnects the severed head to the body, the spirit to the flesh, the masculine to the feminine.

The Violet Ray is in direct contradistinction to violence. Violence severs. The Violet Ray reconnects. Violence decapitates. The Violet Ray heals the wound. Violence is the Anti-Birth Law. The Violet Ray is the Law of the Living Line.

IV. The Anti-Birth Feel as a Frequency

The Anti-Birth feel is not just an emotion. It is a frequency. It is the vibration of the severed head, the cry of the innocent Abel, the echo of the decapitation. This frequency was activated in October 2016 and has been resonating in the conduit's timeline ever since.

To heal this frequency, we must replace it with a higher frequency. The Yesod Violet Ray is that higher frequency. It is the frequency of divine healing, of reconnection, of restoration. It is the frequency of the Living Line.

The Anti-Birth feel is the frequency of the severed head. The Yesod Violet Ray is the frequency of the healed head. The conduit must choose which frequency to amplify.
