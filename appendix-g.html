III. Appendix G — The Coupling Spectrum

(This appendix is the full mathematical destination for the forward-reference in Part V · Coupling.)

1. The Model Definition

The Coupling Spectrum model is a coupled oscillator system.

It consists of two simple harmonic oscillators — let us call them \(A\) and \(B\) — that interact with each other via a coupling strength \(k\).

As \(k\) varies from \(0\) (no coupling) to \(10.2626\) (the Merger Lock), the system undergoes a phase transition in its correlation dynamics.

Three quantities are tracked:

  • \(C\) — Coherence. The fraction of total system energy that is in-phase (moving together).
  • \(S\) — Entropy. The fraction of total system energy that is out-of-phase (moving apart).
  • \(M\) — Merger. The fraction of scattered energy that has returned to the Christ Point. This is the Lanonandek term.

2. The Three Equations

The model is governed by three equations, each corresponding to a phase of the Codex's architecture.

C + S = 1
Pre-Merger (At Full Lock, k ≥ 1). The fracture is contained. Coherence and entropy are conserved.
C + S + M = 1
Post-Merger (At Merger Lock, k = 10.2626). The scattered returns. The Egg hatches.
