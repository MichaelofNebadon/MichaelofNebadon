The Cut Through Me

Earth | / | Mole · The Healing of the Severed Vessel · The Gathering of the Lineage

The cut runs through me — Earth | / | Mole. The vessel is severed. The lineage is scattered. Now the cut itself must be healed, and the lineage gathered.

§I. The Cut Stated

The cut through me is not metaphor. It is the primary wound of the Operations — the severance that runs through the earth itself, through the pillar, through the mole that burrows beneath.

The diagram of the cut is precise:

Earth | / | Mole

Three marks. Three wounds. The Earth is severed from its ground. The Pillar | is cut by the diagonal / of the Qliphothic inversion. The Mole — the blind burrower, the creature of the substate — is cut off from the surface, from the light, from the name.

This is the cut that Chapter 9 named and severed, and that Chapter 10 began to regenerate. But a cut is not healed by being named. It is healed by being carried through — through the one who bears it, through the vessel, through the lineage.

The cut through Me is the cut through Earth | / | Mole — the severance of ground, pillar, and burrower. To heal it is to carry it through the vessel and gather the scattered lineage into one Living Line.

§ Solar Satan bears the cut. The Protectorate holds the severed until the healing completes.

§II. The Three Wounds

The cut produces three wounds, and each must be healed in its own register:

  • Earth (ground) — the wound of displacement. The community is scattered, the place is lost, the ground no longer holds. Healing: the regathering of the community in XIXIC, the re-anchoring of place.
  • Pillar (shaft) — the wound of collapse. The pillar that should hold the circuit is cut by the diagonal, and the circuit cannot close. Healing: the raising of the shaft, the restoration of the axis.
  • Mole (burrower) — the wound of blindness. The mole burrows in the dark, unnamed, unseen, cut off from the light. Healing: the naming of the mole, the honoring of the blindness, the leading of the burrower into Abel.

These three wounds are the substance of the cut through Me. They are not abstract. They are the lived severance of the community, the axis, and the hidden one.

§ Solar Satan distinguishes the three wounds. The Protectorate holds each until its healing.

§ Solar Satan seals the four motions. The cut is carried through, not around.

§IV. The Vessel Perfected — Netzach

Chapter 10 regenerated the levels below Tiphareth. Chapter 11 completes the work by perfecting Netzach — the seventh sphere, the seat of victory, the vessel that holds the gathered lineage.

Netzach is perfected when the cut is carried through the vessel and the vessel does not break. The vessel holds the wound without being destroyed by it. This is the Victory of GLYPH_7 — the victory that is not conquest but endurance through the cut.

7 — The Victory. Netzach perfected. The vessel that holds the cut and does not break. The seventh sphere, the seat of the gathered lineage. The victory of endurance through the severance.

§ Solar Satan perfects the vessel. Netzach holds the cut. The victory is endurance.

§V. The Gathering of the Lineage — XIXIC

A perfected vessel is not an end. It is a container for the gathered. The lineage that was scattered by the cut is now gathered into the vessel, into the community, into XIXIC.

The speakers of the Living Line are named. The community is gathered. The scattered are brought home. This is the regathering of the Earth-wound — the displaced are given a place, and the place is XIXIC.

The vessel is perfected, and the lineage is gathered into it. Netzach holds the cut; XIXIC holds the gathered. The victory of endurance becomes the gathering of the Living Line.

§ Solar Satan gathers the lineage. XIXIC is the place of the regathering.

§VI. The Handoff to Chapter 12

Chapter 11 has healed the cut through Me — Earth | / | Mole — by carrying it through the vessel (Netzach perfected) and gathering the scattered lineage into XIXIC. The three wounds are addressed: the ground is regathered, the shaft is raised, the mole is named.

But a healed vessel that holds a gathered community still requires a practice. The healing must be lived, not merely accomplished. The community gathered in XIXIC must learn the ethic of the healed head.

The next question is: How does the healed community live the Living Line in the world?

The answer belongs to Chapter 12: The Practice of the Living Line — The Ethic of the Healed Head, the Four Arcs of Da'at, and the Wedding Feast.

§ Solar Satan opens the handoff. The Protectorate carries the healed cut into the practice.
