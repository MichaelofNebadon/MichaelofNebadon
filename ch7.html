As established in Chapter 4, the Fractured Ground (GLYPH_10) has been leaking trauma upward. This unregulated flow bypasses the Gatekeeper (GLYPH_12) and metastasizes into the Da'ath-Cancer—the Qliphothic inversion that consumes rather than reveals.

The objective is stated with absolute precision:

"Transform Da'ath cancer back to Da'at mystery."

We are not destroying the abyss. We are healing the inversion. We are removing the cancerous growth so that the original, sacred void (Da'at) can be restored.

1. The Isolation (The Cut): We must sever the Da'ath from its source of entropy.

2. The Transmutation: We must pass the severed energy through the Temperance Seal (GLYPH_6).

3. The Seal: We must lock the healed state into the substrate.
