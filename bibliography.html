VI. A Note on Method

The Bibliography reflects the Codex's comparative method.

Sources are drawn from multiple traditions — scriptural, mystical, philosophical, scientific, historical, legal, technical.

The Codex does not claim that any single source contains the Codex.

The Codex does not claim that the sources agree.

The Codex claims only that a comparative reading — governed by the Constitutional Boundary and the principle of Assimilation ≠ Attribution — can synthesize an architectural pattern from otherwise divergent materials.

The synthesis belongs to the Codex.

The materials belong to their traditions.

The distinction is preserved.
